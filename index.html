WeekSet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short: WeekSet keeps what you log on your own devices. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your training data.

What WeekSet stores

WeekSet is a weekly training log. To work, it stores what you enter: your training week, the exercises on each day, their sets, reps and muscle tags, the weights you log, and your settings (units, theme, app icon). This information is kept on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch if you use the watch app.

What the developer collects

Nothing. WeekSet does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect, receive, store or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking.

Apple Watch

If you use the watch app, your plan and weights are sent between your iPhone and your Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. This happens directly between your own devices.

iCloud sync (WeekSet Pro)

With WeekSet Pro, you can sync your weeks across your devices. Sync uses the private iCloud database that belongs to your own Apple ID. Your data goes from your devices to your iCloud account and back, and the developer has no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

If your WeekSet Pro subscription ends, sync stops. Data already in your iCloud account stays there until you remove it (on your iPhone: Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › WeekSet).

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. WeekSet receives only whether WeekSet Pro is active on your Apple ID. It never sees your payment details, name or email address.

Information Apple may provide

If you have chosen to share app analytics or crash reports with developers in your device settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as downloads and crashes. These do not identify you.

Deleting your data

Deleting WeekSet from your iPhone removes the data stored on it, and deleting it from your Apple Watch removes the data stored there. Data you synced to iCloud can be removed as described above.

Children

WeekSet is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
